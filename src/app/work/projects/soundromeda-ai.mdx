On Soundromeda’s company people / org graph I sat under the CEO as **Head of Artificial Intelligence** — after joining as founding AI engineer.

## Company context

Soundromeda is a music-gaming studio / AI research lab / UGC platform — turn songs into Unreal-powered “songworlds,” with artists owning their worlds. Leadership around the CEO (Brayden Pierce) includes gaming, narrative, environment art, and BD; AI sat as a first-class leadership seat on that bench.

## Highlights

- Agentic / AI systems for music-world creation and product acceleration
- Creator-first narrative: songs as worlds, artists as solar systems, music as galactic gameplay
- Public milestone while in the AI seat: Soundromeda joined the **NVIDIA Inception** startup program
