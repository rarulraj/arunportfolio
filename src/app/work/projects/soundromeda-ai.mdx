## Role

On Soundromeda’s company people graph I sat under the CEO as **Head of Artificial Intelligence**, after joining as the founding AI engineer.

- **Founding Artificial Intelligence Engineer** · Jan 2022 – Jan 2024. Built the AI product from zero, helped secure funding, and put machine learning into the product instead of leaving it as a research deck.
- **Head of Artificial Intelligence** · Dec 2023 – Mar 2024. Led the AI team, set a single product vision, and shipped systems artists and players actually touched.

The public org bench is on the [Soundromeda people page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/soundromeda/people/).

## The artists surface

The page I want this case study to point at is [soundromeda.com/artists](https://www.soundromeda.com/artists) — “Soundromeda /// Founding Artists.” It is the front door for musicians, not a generic marketing homepage.

The pitch on that page is specific. Streams, reels, and generative clips are not the whole market. Soundromeda’s line is to reach the billions of people who already play games, with something that is not only an audio-reactive visualizer. It is a music video game with action-adventure gameplay: a song becomes a world you can move through.

Under that, **Claim your world / Apply for access** is a real intake, not a mailto link. An artist submits:

- Stage name, main genre, and a link (EPK, site, or Instagram)
- One song to share
- Whether they control licensing rights, can secure them, or a label handles them
- A favorite video game, who referred them, email, and region

![Claim your world — artist access form on soundromeda.com/artists](/images/work/soundromeda/claim.jpg)

That form is the product thesis in one screen. Rights, a song, and a game reference are the inputs for turning a catalog into a playable world. The footer line on the same page calls Soundromeda the audiovisual world-builder for sharing and monetizing music.

## What the worlds look like

Songworlds are Unreal environments, not lyric videos. The artists page shows playable scenes: a figure moving through a neon cavern, a gallery room built like a music video set, action-adventure combat staged inside the track.

![Soundromeda gameplay — a song staged as an action-adventure world](/images/work/soundromeda/gameplay.jpg)

Artists own their worlds. The studio language around the product is songs as worlds, artists as solar systems, music as something you play rather than only stream. Leadership next to the CEO covered gaming, narrative, environment art, and business development. AI was a first-class seat on that bench, not a vendor bolted on after the game was already designed.

## What I owned

My job was the AI layer that makes that loop faster and more coherent:

- Systems that help turn music and creator intent into world-building inputs, so a song is not hand-translated into a level by guesswork alone
- A single AI vision across creator tools and player-facing surfaces, instead of a pile of one-off models
- Shipping through the period when Soundromeda joined the **NVIDIA Inception** startup program

The work sat between research and production. Models had to survive inside a game studio: Unreal worlds, artist ownership, and a creator funnel that starts with a song and a rights question.
