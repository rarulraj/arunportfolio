## Challenge

OT operators shouldn’t context-switch out of the historian to diagnose a turbine. Geospatial asset state, high-frequency telemetry, and agentic reasoning need a single semantic surface — not a GIS tab, a SCADA HMI, and a chat window in three different tools.

## Architecture

- **CesiumJS** globe / 3D tileset of Ridge Wind embedded as an **IDMP canvas panel** (WGS84 positions, entity picking, status-driven materials)
- **Asset hierarchy** in IDMP: customer → site → string → turbine (`WT-N01`…`WT-S03`) with attributes, limits, and child elements
- **TSDB binding**: active power (kW), wind speed (m/s), rotor RPM, nacelle temperature streamed into panel widgets and floating entity labels
- **Operational state machine**: healthy / curtailed / fault rendered as emerald · amber · red entity styling on the globe
- **Agentic diagnosis** on the canvas: live summary, panel interpretation, and evidence retrieval (breaker trip vs aerodynamic scatter loss) via the **knowledge graph**, not free-form hallucination

## What I shipped

- Seven-turbine Cesium scene with pickable entities and HUD overlays (fleet overwatch + per-asset telemetry cards)
- Side-by-side dashboards for string-level MW totals, meteorological context, and related signals
- Canvas-native AI workflow: select asset → inspect panels → agent cites evidence from the semantic model
- Demo narrative for ProveIt / LinkedIn / customer workshops: **Data → Context → Action**

## Outcome

A reusable industrial AI reference architecture — time-series historian + asset contextualization + geospatial digital twin + grounded agent — used in enterprise engagements to show why TDengine IDMP is more than tags and charts.
