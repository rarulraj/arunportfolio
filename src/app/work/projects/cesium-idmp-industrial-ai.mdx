## Challenge

Operators shouldn’t leave the historian to diagnose a turbine. Spatial state, live metrics, and AI reasoning need to live in one place.

## What I shipped

- Cesium map of seven wind turbines embedded as an IDMP canvas panel
- Color-coded ops state: green healthy · yellow curtailed · red fault
- Embedded dashboards beside the map (power, wind speed, related signals)
- Agentic diagnosis from the canvas — live summary, panel interpretation, evidence (e.g. breaker trip vs scatter loss) via the knowledge graph

## Outcome

A demo narrative used across LinkedIn and customer conversations: **Data → Context → Action** — time-series + asset hierarchy + real context + streaming insight, not tags alone.
