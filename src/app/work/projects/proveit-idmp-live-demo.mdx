## Challenge

Show a modern historian story live — asset hierarchy, context, streaming insights — without collapsing into a slideware pitch.

## What I shipped

- Live IDMP walkthrough at ProveIt: models, panels, and AI-assisted interpretation on industrial data
- Demo surfaces spanning equipment views (e.g. evaporator / pump context), dashboards, and conversational analysis
- Follow-up narrative: most historians store data; we built TDengine to understand it

## Outcome

Strong post-event engagement on the live demo. Featured on my LinkedIn profile as a flagship industrial AI moment for TDengine.
