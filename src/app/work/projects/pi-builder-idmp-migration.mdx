## Challenge

Teams moving off classic PI-style tooling need a concrete path into IDMP — not just “it’s possible,” but import tabs, connection model, and where capabilities map or gap.

## What I shipped

- Customer pack: bulk import guide (flow diagrams, connection/import tabs, capability-gap framing)
- Visuals that make the PI Builder → IDMP journey scannable in demos and enablement sessions
- Packaging meant for handoff to customers and internal solution engineering

## Outcome

A reusable enablement artifact for historian modernization conversations — shorter time from “interested” to “we can see how our structure lands in IDMP.”
